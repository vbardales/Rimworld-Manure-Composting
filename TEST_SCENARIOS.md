# Functional validation scenarios

Nothing here is left as a manual test. Each scenario below is either **automated** (played by the Pickle suite in `Tests/Pickle/`,
or proven offline) or **not applicable**, with its reason. The passes, the conditions to reach `tested` and the evidence to keep are in
[TESTING.md](TESTING.md). None of the automated in-game scenarios has been played yet: they are written, and their vocabulary is
checked (`Tests/Pickle/Check-Steps.ps1`).

Common prerequisites: RimWorld 1.6, Harmony as required by Manure, Burok.Manure, Dubwise.DubsBadHygiene, then
nelim.manurecomposting. The DLL and file hashes are in `Tests/Results/artifact-hashes.csv` (on disk, not in git). English and French are
played in separate launches.

After every in-game scenario the startup `Player.log` is checked for errors, missing translations or defs, exceptions and repeated messages
(`TESTING.md`, condition 6). A green report says the path ran; it does not say that a capture shows anything, which is why the `@review`
captures are opened.
