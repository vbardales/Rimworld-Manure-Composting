## Code review, before the passes had run

A `/code-review` since `0.1.0` found one real defect in the new suite, in `Tests/Pickle/Source/ManureCompostingSteps.cs`: `Held()` treated a fermented composter
as an automatic mismatch (returned -1), but DBH advances `Progress` on a fixed schedule once any material is present, independent of fill level, so a scenario
could in principle finish cooking before the pawn hauled in the full amount, and the assertion would then fail although the mod worked. Fixed by replacing
`Held()` with `HoldsAtLeast()`, which treats a fermented composter as having received the expected amount when none of the scenario's spawned Manure is left
unhauled on the map. Rebuilt (0 errors) and rechecked with `Check-Steps.ps1` (9 patterns, 62 step lines, green). Arithmetic check on DBH's constants
(`pertick=3.3333e-6`, rare tick every 250 ticks) puts full fermentation at roughly 300,000 ticks, far beyond what a single-trip haul or the 40-second real-time
assertion window would reach in practice, so the original race was unlikely to trigger in this suite's scenarios, but the fix removes the dependency on that
margin entirely.
